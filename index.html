01 · MY JOURNEY

From a small community idea to a broader leadership practice.

My early work began around learning opportunities and community support. Over time, that practical experience grew into school programmes, emergency response, volunteer coordination, women's empowerment initiatives and year-round welfare activities.

What matters to me now is the learning behind those activities: how programmes are designed, how people coordinate during uncertainty, how resources are managed, and how community initiatives can become more sustainable.

The next stage is to turn practical leadership experience into stronger analytical and strategic capability.
